# Unit Economics Benchmarks

## Purpose

Stage and sector-specific benchmarks for evaluating startup unit economics. Use these as reference points, not absolute standards — context always matters.

---

## SaaS Benchmarks by Stage

### Pre-Seed / Seed

| Metric | Acceptable | Good | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | $10K-$50K | $50K-$150K | >$150K |
| MoM (Month-over-Month) growth | 10-15% | 15-25% | >25% |
| Gross margin | >60% | >70% | >80% |
| Burn multiple | <4x | <2.5x | <1.5x |
| CAC payback | <24 months | <18 months | <12 months |

### Series A

| Metric | Acceptable | Good | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) | $1M-$2M | $2M-$5M | >$5M |
| YoY (Year-over-Year) growth | 2x | 2.5-3x | >3x |
| Gross margin | >65% | >75% | >80% |
| LTV/CAC | >2x | >3x | >5x |
| NRR (Net Revenue Retention) | >100% | >110% | >130% |
| Burn multiple | <3x | <2x | <1.5x |
| Logo churn (monthly) | <5% | <3% | <1.5% |

### Series B

| Metric | Acceptable | Good | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR | $5M-$10M | $10M-$25M | >$25M |
| YoY growth | 1.5-2x | 2-2.5x | >2.5x |
| Gross margin | >70% | >75% | >85% |
| LTV/CAC | >3x | >4x | >6x |
| NRR | >105% | >120% | >140% |
| Burn multiple | <2.5x | <1.5x | <1x |
| Rule of 40 (growth % + margin %) | >20 | >40 | >60 |

## SaaS Benchmarks by Segment

### SMB (Small and Medium Business) SaaS

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ACV (Annual Contract Value) | $1K-$25K | Below $1K is self-serve consumer-like |
| CAC | $500-$5K | Product-led growth can push CAC toward $0 |
| CAC payback | 6-12 months | SMB must payback fast due to higher churn |
| Monthly logo churn | 2-5% | Higher than enterprise — expected |
| NRR | 90-110% | Expansion harder with smaller customers |
| Sales cycle | 1-4 weeks | Longer = red flag for SMB |

### Mid-Market SaaS

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ACV | $25K-$100K | Sweet spot for efficient growth |
| CAC | $10K-$50K | Inside sales or light field sales |
| CAC payback | 12-18 months | Acceptable given lower churn |
| Monthly logo churn | 1-2% | Should be noticeably lower than SMB |
| NRR | 105-125% | Expansion through seats, modules, usage |
| Sales cycle | 1-3 months | |

### Enterprise SaaS

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ACV | $100K-$1M+ | Below $100K is mid-market |
| CAC | $50K-$500K | Field sales, long cycles, POCs (Proofs of Concept) |
| CAC payback | 18-24 months | Acceptable due to very low churn |
| Annual logo churn | 5-10% | Monthly churn misleading — measure annually |
| NRR | 115-150% | Enterprise expansion is the growth engine |
| Sales cycle | 3-12 months | |

## Cross-Cutting Metrics

### Magic Number (Sales Efficiency)

**Formula:** Net new ARR in quarter / Sales & marketing spend in prior quarter

| Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| <0.5 | Inefficient — spending too much to acquire revenue |
| 0.5-0.75 | Acceptable at early stage |
| 0.75-1.0 | Good — efficient growth |
| >1.0 | Excellent — invest more in sales & marketing |

### Hype Ratio

**Formula:** Valuation / ARR (or annual revenue)

| Stage | Typical Range | Justified If |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-seed | 50-200x | Team and market are exceptional |
| Seed | 20-80x | Strong early traction |
| Series A | 15-50x | Clear PMF (Product-Market Fit), strong growth |
| Series B | 10-30x | Proven unit economics, scaling |
| Growth | 5-20x | Depends on growth rate and margin |

### Gross Margin by Business Type

| Type | Expected Gross Margin |
|---|---|
| Pure SaaS | 75-90% |
| SaaS + services | 55-70% |
| Marketplace | 60-80% (on net revenue) |
| Hardware + software | 30-50% |
| Fintech (transaction) | 40-60% |
| Deep tech / biotech | Variable (often negative pre-revenue) |
