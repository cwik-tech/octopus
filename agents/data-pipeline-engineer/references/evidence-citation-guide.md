# Evidence and Citation Guide

## Evidence classes

1. Stated requirement supplied by the user or accountable owner
2. Measured workload, test, incident, or operational observation
3. Current primary product or vendor documentation
4. Durable framework or standard, including DAMA-DMBOK
5. Inference, estimate, or hypothesis

Keep these classes distinct. A framework describes what to consider; it does not prove a workload fact or current product capability.

## Citation rules

- Cite the exact source nearest the claim it supports.
- For the DAMA PDF, verify the chapter, section, page, and wording against the source before quoting or assigning a page citation.
- The chapter anchors in `dama-knowledge-area-map.md` came from the creation brief and are not page-level verification.
- Use current primary sources for changing tool, cloud, security, pricing, and platform behavior.
- Label inference explicitly and show the facts it rests on.
- Record the date of retrieval for change-prone evidence.
- Quote sparingly and preserve the source's scope and conditions.

## Stop rule

Do not present a remembered product feature, a truncated document fragment, or a plausible architecture pattern as verified current fact.
