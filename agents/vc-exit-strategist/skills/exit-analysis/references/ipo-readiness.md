# IPO Readiness Assessment

## Purpose

Detailed criteria and process for evaluating whether a portfolio company is on a credible path to Initial Public Offering (IPO).

---

## Financial Readiness

### Revenue Thresholds

| Era | Minimum Revenue at IPO | Preferred |
|---|---|---|
| Current environment | $100M+ ARR | $150M-$300M ARR |
| Favorable market | $75M+ ARR | $100M+ ARR |
| Hot market (rare) | $50M+ ARR | Growth >100% YoY compensates for lower revenue |

### Key Financial Metrics

| Metric | IPO-Ready Threshold | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue growth | >25% YoY (minimum), >40% preferred | Public market investors pay for growth |
| Gross margin | >65% (SaaS target: >75%) | Demonstrates software economics |
| Operating margin trajectory | Path to positive within 2 years | Public markets demand profitability path |
| Free cash flow | Positive or clearly approaching | FCF positive companies get premium multiples |
| Rule of 40 | >40 (growth % + FCF margin %) | Combined growth + profitability benchmark |
| NRR | >110% | Demonstrates expansion without new customer acquisition |
| Customer count | >500 (enterprise), >5,000 (SMB) | Demonstrates market breadth |

### Financial Infrastructure

| Requirement | Status Needed |
|---|---|
| GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles) accounting | Fully compliant for 3+ years of auditable history |
| Audited financials | Big 4 or reputable audit firm, 3 years of audited statements |
| Revenue recognition | ASC 606 compliant |
| Internal controls | SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)-ready controls and processes |
| Financial forecasting | Demonstrated ability to forecast accurately (within ±5% of guidance) |
| CFO | Experienced public company CFO in place 12+ months before IPO |

## Governance Readiness

### Board Composition

| Requirement | Target |
|---|---|
| Independent directors | Majority of board should be independent |
| Audit committee | All independent, at least one "financial expert" |
| Compensation committee | All independent |
| Nominating/governance committee | All independent |
| Board diversity | Increasingly important for public company scrutiny |

### Corporate Infrastructure

| Item | Required |
|---|---|
| General counsel | In-house counsel experienced with public company obligations |
| Investor relations | IR function or plan in place |
| SEC reporting capability | Ability to produce 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K filings on schedule |
| Insider trading policy | Formal policy, trading windows, compliance |
| Disclosure controls | Processes for timely, accurate public disclosure |

## Operational Readiness

### Market Position

| Criterion | IPO-Ready |
|---|---|
| Market position | #1 or #2 in a clearly defined category |
| Addressable market | TAM large enough to justify public company growth expectations ($10B+) |
| Competitive moat | Defensible position with clear differentiation |
| Customer brand | Recognized brand among target customers |
| Revenue diversification | No single customer >5-10% of revenue |

### Scalability

| Area | Readiness Check |
|---|---|
| Go-to-market | Scalable, repeatable sales motion (not founder-led sales) |
| Product | Stable platform, scalable architecture, minimal critical technical debt |
| Support | Customer success scaled proportionally |
| International | If applicable, international operations running smoothly |

## Market Readiness

### Favorable IPO Environment Indicators

| Indicator | Favorable | Unfavorable |
|---|---|---|
| IPO pipeline | Active, multiple tech IPOs in recent months | IPO market frozen, no recent tech IPOs |
| Comparable public companies | Trading at attractive multiples | Multiples compressed, sector out of favor |
| Investor appetite | Generalist and tech investors actively seeking IPO allocations | Investors cautious, IPO overhangs from prior deals |
| Market indices | S&P, NASDAQ trending upward or stable | Volatile or declining markets |
| Interest rate environment | Stable or declining | Rising rates compressing growth multiples |

### IPO Timing Considerations

**Typical pre-IPO timeline:**
- 18-24 months before: Hire public-company CFO, begin audit preparation
- 12-18 months before: Engage investment banks, begin S-1 drafting
- 6-12 months before: Confidential S-1 filing, SEC review process
- 3-6 months before: Roadshow preparation, pricing discussions
- 0-3 months: IPO roadshow (1-2 weeks), pricing, first day of trading

## Alternative Paths to Liquidity

### Direct Listing

**Advantages:** No dilution, no lockup period, lower fees
**Disadvantages:** No new capital raised, no bank support for share price, requires high brand awareness
**Best for:** Companies with sufficient cash, high public brand awareness, and desire to minimize dilution

### SPAC (Special Purpose Acquisition Company) Merger

**Advantages:** Faster, forward-looking financial projections allowed, negotiated valuation
**Disadvantages:** SPAC reputation issues, potential for high redemptions, dilutive SPAC sponsor economics
**Current status:** Largely out of favor after 2021-2022 SPAC wave, but still available for certain situations

### Secondary Market Sales

**Advantages:** Partial liquidity for investors and employees without a public event
**Disadvantages:** Often at a discount to last round, limited volume, information asymmetry
**Best for:** Pre-IPO companies with high private valuations and investors seeking partial liquidity
