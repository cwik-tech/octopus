# Historical Exit Multiples

## Purpose

Reference data on exit multiples by sector, acquirer type, and exit type. Use as benchmarks when modeling exit scenarios for current deals.

---

## Strategic Acquisition Multiples by Sector

*Note: Multiples vary significantly based on growth rate, profitability, strategic fit, and competitive dynamics. These are ranges, not precise numbers.*

### SaaS / Enterprise Software

| Buyer Motivation | Typical EV/Revenue Multiple | When This Multiple Applies |
|---|---|---|
| Technology acquisition (acqui-hire plus) | 2-5x | Small company, valuable tech, limited revenue |
| Product acquisition (bolt-on) | 5-10x | Strong product, fills gap in acquirer's portfolio |
| Market/customer acquisition | 8-15x | Established customer base, strategic customers |
| Strategic acquisition (competitive) | 10-20x+ | Multiple bidders, must-have asset |

**Key driver:** Growth rate. A SaaS company growing >50% YoY commands 2-3x the multiple of one growing 20%.

### Marketplace / Platform

| Buyer Motivation | Typical EV/Revenue Multiple | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Market expansion | 3-8x (on net revenue) | Acquirer wants geographic or category access |
| Network acquisition | 5-15x (on net revenue) | Buying the network effect — premium if defensible |
| Competitive elimination | 8-20x+ | Removing a competitor — highest premium |

**Key driver:** Network defensibility. A marketplace with strong network effects commands significantly higher multiples.

### Fintech

| Buyer Motivation | Typical Multiple | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Technology / license | 3-8x revenue | Buying technology stack or regulatory licenses |
| Customer base | 5-12x revenue | Buying customer relationships and data |
| Strategic (bank/insurer acquiring fintech) | 8-20x revenue | Banks pay premium for digital capabilities |

### Healthtech

| Buyer Motivation | Typical Multiple | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Technology / data | 5-10x revenue | Proprietary data or algorithms |
| Provider network | 3-8x revenue | Access to healthcare providers or patients |
| Compliance / regulatory | 8-15x+ revenue | FDA-cleared or HIPAA-compliant solutions at premium |

### Consumer / D2C

| Buyer Motivation | Typical Multiple | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brand acquisition | 1-4x revenue | Consumer brands rarely command high multiples |
| User base acquisition | 2-6x revenue | Measured per user, not per revenue $ |
| Strategic (distribution) | 3-8x revenue | If distribution channel is unique |

## IPO Multiples by Sector

| Sector | Median IPO EV/Revenue (at IPO) | Range | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS (high growth >40%) | 15-25x | 10-40x | $100M+ ARR, strong NRR, path to profitability |
| SaaS (moderate growth 20-40%) | 8-15x | 5-20x | Profitability or near-profitability required |
| Marketplace | 8-15x (on net revenue) | 5-25x | Depends on take rate and market structure |
| Fintech | 10-20x | 5-30x | Highly variable based on growth and risk |
| Consumer subscription | 5-12x | 3-20x | Depends on retention and growth |
| Hardware + software | 3-8x | 2-15x | Lower multiples due to lower margins |

## PE (Private Equity) Buyout Multiples

| Company Profile | Typical EBITDA Multiple | When This Applies |
|---|---|---|
| Stable, low-growth SaaS | 10-15x EBITDA | Profitable, <20% growth, can support leverage |
| High-growth SaaS (PE growth equity) | 15-25x EBITDA | 20-40% growth, improving margins |
| Marketplace / transactional | 8-12x EBITDA | Stable take rates, moderate growth |
| Services-heavy tech | 6-10x EBITDA | Lower multiples due to labor intensity |

## Factors That Drive Multiple Premiums

| Factor | Premium Effect | Magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| Growth rate | Each 10% of growth adds ~1-2x multiple | Significant |
| Net revenue retention >120% | Signals expansion and stickiness | +2-4x multiple |
| Gross margin >80% | Signals software economics | +1-3x multiple |
| Category leader (#1 position) | Brand premium and lower customer acquisition risk | +2-5x multiple |
| Multiple strategic bidders | Competitive tension drives price up | +20-50% premium |
| Proprietary data / network effects | Defensibility premium | +2-5x multiple |
| Recurring revenue (vs. transactional) | Predictability premium | +3-5x multiple |
| Rule of 40 score >40 | Growth + profitability combined | +2-4x multiple |

---

## Factors That Drive Multiple Discounts

| Factor | Discount Effect | Magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| Customer concentration (top 3 >30%) | Single-customer risk | -2-5x multiple |
| Declining growth rate | Decelerating momentum | -3-5x multiple |
| Gross margin <60% | Questions about software economics | -2-4x multiple |
| Regulatory risk | Uncertain future economics | -2-5x multiple |
| No clear moat | Replaceable product | -3-5x multiple |
| Complex cap table (preference stack) | Reduces common shareholder value | -1-3x effective multiple |
