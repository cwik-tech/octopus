# Data Security Control Baseline

## Classification and minimization

- Identify sensitive fields and the authority for classification.
- Collect, copy, expose, and retain only what the stated purpose requires.

## Identity and access

- Authenticate every principal and authorize each action at the boundary.
- Use least privilege, separate human and service identities, review access, and control break-glass use.

## Protection

- Encrypt in transit and at rest across primary stores, replicas, backups, logs, exports, and temporary files.
- Manage secrets outside source and artifacts; scope, rotate, and audit them.
- Apply masking, tokenization, aggregation, or synthetic data according to consumer need.

## Lifecycle

- Define retention, legal hold, deletion, and propagation to derived data, caches, backups, and non-production environments.
- Verify migration and incident procedures do not create uncontrolled copies.

## Audit and response

- Record access, privilege changes, exports, destructive operations, and policy overrides without recording protected values.
- Define owner, response route, containment, evidence preservation, and notification obligations.

## Escalation

This baseline is screening only. Escalate legal interpretation, privacy impact assessment, threat modeling, penetration testing, cryptographic design, infrastructure security, and certification.
