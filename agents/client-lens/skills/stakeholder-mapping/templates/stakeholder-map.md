## Engagement Strategy by Stakeholder Type

**Decision Makers:**
- Cadence: Weekly steering or decision-gate meetings.
- Format: Structured updates with clear yes/no/defer options.
- Content: Business case, risks, trade-offs, interdependencies.
- Escalation trigger: [Describe when to escalate].

**Champions & Influencers:**
- Cadence: Bi-weekly strategy sessions or as needed.
- Format: Co-design opportunities, feedback solicitation, public recognition.
- Content: Strategic alignment, peer examples, how to advocate internally.
- Ask: Use them to broker relationships with skeptics.

**Blockers:**
- Cadence: 1-1 meetings to understand concerns before they harden.
- Format: Listen first; propose options that address their incentives.
- Content: Evidence, risk mitigation, win-win framing.
- Approach: Address hidden agendas directly; don't assume stated objections are the real ones.

**Budget Holders:**
- Cadence: Monthly financial reviews.
- Format: ROI metrics, cost tracking, milestone-based releases of funds.
- Content: Financial summaries, risk-adjusted costs, total cost of ownership (TCO).
- Leverage: Tie milestones to budget gates (e.g., "Phase 2 funding released only if Phase 1 achieves X metric").

**End Users:**
- Cadence: Pilot feedback loops; working sessions during design.
- Format: Hands-on demos, feedback forms, change impact briefings.
- Content: How-to guides, FAQ, support availability.
- Leverage: Use their feedback to prove value and generate peer adoption.

**Influencers (Informal Authority):**
- Cadence: Early briefings before formal announcements.
- Format: One-on-one conversations, peer example stories.
- Content: Why this matters, how it helps their domain, how to talk about it.
- Leverage: Ask them to vouch for you with their peers.

## Success Criteria

Stakeholder engagement is successful when:
- [ ] All Decision Makers have approved the initiative scope and approach.
- [ ] No high-power stakeholder is actively opposing (skepticism is acceptable; blocking is not).
- [ ] Champions have visible presence in early phases; momentum is visible.
- [ ] Budget holders have signed off on funding and milestone gates.
- [ ] End users understand the change and see their feedback reflected in design.
- [ ] No critical information gaps remain.
