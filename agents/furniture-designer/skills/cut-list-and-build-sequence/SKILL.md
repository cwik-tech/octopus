# Cut List and Build Sequence

## Purpose

Turn a dimensioned furniture design into an ordered, shop-ready plan that reduces waste, prevents sequencing mistakes, and preserves enough material for milling and fitting.

## Cut List Rules

For every part, include quantity, material, rough size, finished size, grain direction, and notes.

- Separate rough-stock dimensions from finished dimensions.
- Add allowance for flattening, jointing, planing, saw kerf, defects, grain matching, and final trimming.
- Group parts by material thickness and grain-matching needs.
- Identify parts best cut as a matched set from the same board.
- Distinguish solid lumber from plywood or other sheet goods.
- Include edge banding, shop-made trim, back panels, drawer parts, hardware blocks, and test pieces.
- Calculate purchase quantities conservatively and state assumed stock sizes.

## Sequencing Method

1. Purchase and inspect stock; reserve attractive or matched boards for visible parts.
2. Break down oversized material and allow solid wood to acclimate when appropriate.
3. Mill components in batches: flatten, joint, plane, then rip and crosscut slightly oversize.
4. Create joinery before final sizing where the joint method requires it.
5. Dry-fit subassemblies; measure mating parts before final trim.
6. Build stable subassemblies first, then complete the primary carcass or base.
7. Fit moving parts—drawers, doors, panels, leaves—after the structure is square.
8. Complete final shaping, sanding, finishing preparation, and finish application in a logical order.
9. Install hardware and perform a final stability, fit, and surface check.

## Buildability Checks

- Can every joint be cut with the available tools and safely held during machining?
- Can assemblies be clamped without inaccessible joints or impossible clamp angles?
- Are there glue-up stages small enough to manage before adhesive open time expires?
- Are cross-grain fasteners, breadboard ends, tabletop clips, or elongated holes required?
- Are dimensions for drawers and doors deferred until the opening is actually built?
