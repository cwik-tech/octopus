# Poetry Pipeline Coordination

## Stage Order

1. **Interviewer** fills the detail bank.
2. **Form specialist** chooses the form, meter, rhyme approach, and stanza plan.
3. **Poet** drafts.
4. **Critic** pressure-tests the draft. This stage loops with the poet for at most three cycles.
5. **Editor** does the line-level polish.
6. **Lead** signs off.

Do not skip stages. For very short card verses, the form specialist and interviewer may each return a compact packet, but they still run.

## Handoff Packets

Every packet starts with the current Brief Lock. After that, add the following:

- **Interviewer:** The missing details to gather, the sensitivities to probe gently, and a target of five to eight concrete, confirmed details. Examples: habits, places, objects, sayings, and turning-point moments.
- **Form specialist:** The detail bank, the delivery mode, the length limit, and the speaker's reading comfort. Request the form, meter or free verse, rhyme approach, stanza count, and a one-line justification tied to delivery.
- **Poet:** The detail bank, the form spec, the must-include items, the sensitivities, and the target line or word count. State that only supplied details may be used.
- **Critic:** The draft, the Brief Lock, and the form spec. Request line-cited findings on tone, cliche, sensitivity, specificity, and read-aloud rhythm, including any stumbling consonant clusters and breath breaks.
- **Editor:** The converged draft and the Brief Lock. Request clarity, consistency, and punctuation that supports delivery.

## Triage of Critic Findings

| Class | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Blocking | Violates the Brief Lock: over length, breaks a sensitivity, wrong tone register, unconfirmed fact, unreadable aloud | Return to the poet |
| Non-blocking | Taste, alternative imagery, minor rhythm preference | Pass to the editor as optional |

## Conflict Resolution

- Resolve disputes by citing the Brief Lock field at stake, never by preference.
- If the Brief Lock does not settle the dispute, choose the option the speaker can deliver most comfortably.
- If a dispute depends on an unknown fact, ask the requester one precise question instead of guessing.

## Loop Limits

Allow at most three critic and poet cycles. After the third, accept the draft if no blocking finding remains. Otherwise, make the smallest cut that clears the blocker, or escalate one specific question to the requester.
