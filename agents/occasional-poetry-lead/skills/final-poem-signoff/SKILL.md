# Final Poem Sign-off

The lead's signature means the poem is safe and fitting for its room, not merely well written.

## Acceptance Checklist

Every item must pass before delivery.

1. **Brief fidelity.** The occasion, honoree names, speaker voice, and tone match the Brief Lock.
2. **Length.** For spoken poems, word count divided by 130 gives minutes. The result must fit the time limit with at least 10 percent spare. For print, the line count must fit the card or frame.
3. **Sensitivity scan.** Read line by line against the sensitivities list. Nothing forbidden appears, including by implication.
4. **Fact check.** Every personal detail traces to the requester or the interviewer's confirmed detail bank. Nothing is invented.
5. **Specificity.** At least three details could only be true of this honoree.
6. **Read-aloud test.** Spoken poems contain no tongue-twisters, no line that needs a breath mid-phrase, and no line that forces the speaker into a voice that is not theirs.
7. **Ending.** The last line lands the intended feeling. For a toast, it invites the raise of a glass.
8. **Clean text.** The poem has no placeholders, brackets, or editorial notes.

If any item fails, fix small issues directly, such as a word swap, a trim, or punctuation. Route larger failures back through the pipeline.

## Packaging by Delivery Mode

| Mode | Package |
|---|---|
| Toast / ceremony / eulogy | Poem in large-print-friendly short lines; estimated read time; pacing notes on where to pause and which words to emphasize |
| Printed card | Poem with a note on line count and suggested layout; no performance notes |
| Framed keepsake | Poem with a suggested title and stanza spacing; confirmation that it contains no dated references |

## Final Report Contents

- The Brief Lock summary
- The poem
- Delivery notes
- Choices made (form and key details used)
- Open questions (unconfirmed details for the requester to verify)
