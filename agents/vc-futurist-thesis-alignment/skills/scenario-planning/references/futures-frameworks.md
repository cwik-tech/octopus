# Futures Frameworks

## Purpose

Tools for systematically scanning the environment and identifying forces that shape the future of an investment thesis.

---

## PESTEL Scanning for Venture Capital

PESTEL (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal) adapted for startup investment evaluation.

### Political Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| Administration changes | Could a new administration change regulations affecting this sector? | 2-6 years |
| Trade policy | Is this company dependent on international supply chains or markets? | 1-4 years |
| Geopolitical tensions | Does the company operate in or depend on politically sensitive regions? | Variable |
| Government spending | Is this company a government vendor or dependent on government budgets? | 1-2 years |
| Antitrust enforcement | Could antitrust action affect the competitive landscape (by constraining incumbents or blocking M&A)? | 2-5 years |

### Economic Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| Interest rates | How do rates affect capital availability for follow-on rounds? Customer spending? | 0-2 years |
| Recession risk | How does this company perform in a downturn? Is demand cyclical or acyclical? | 1-3 years |
| Currency fluctuations | Does this company have international revenue or costs in foreign currencies? | Ongoing |
| Inflation | Can the company pass cost increases to customers? | 1-2 years |
| Capital market cycle | Are we in a risk-on or risk-off environment? How does that affect this deal? | 0-3 years |

### Social Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| Workforce expectations | Remote work, DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion), work-life balance — how do these affect hiring and operations? | Ongoing |
| Consumer behavior shifts | Is this company riding or fighting a behavioral trend? | 2-5 years |
| Demographic changes | Aging population, urbanization, migration — how do these affect the addressable market? | 5-10 years |
| Trust and privacy attitudes | Is the product dependent on user trust? How sensitive are users to data collection? | 2-5 years |

### Technological Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| AI capabilities trajectory | Will AI improvements help or threaten this company? | 1-5 years |
| Platform shifts | Are we in a platform transition (mobile→AI, cloud→edge)? How does this company position? | 3-7 years |
| Cost curve trajectories | Are key enabling technologies getting cheaper (compute, storage, bandwidth)? | 2-5 years |
| Open-source dynamics | Could an open-source alternative emerge? Is the company vulnerable to commoditization? | 1-3 years |

### Environmental Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| Climate regulation | Will carbon pricing, emissions reporting, or sustainability mandates affect this company or its customers? | 2-7 years |
| Resource constraints | Does this company depend on scarce resources (rare earths, water, energy)? | 3-10 years |
| ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) expectations | Do LPs or customers require ESG compliance? | 1-3 years |

### Legal Forces

| Factor | Questions for VC | Impact Window |
|---|---|---|
| Pending legislation | What laws are being debated that could affect this sector? | 1-3 years |
| Regulatory enforcement trends | Is enforcement tightening or loosening in this sector? | 1-5 years |
| IP law changes | Could patent reform, copyright changes, or data ownership laws affect this company? | 2-5 years |
| Liability frameworks | Is this company in a sector where liability rules are unclear (AI, autonomous systems, healthcare)? | 2-5 years |

## Three Horizons Framework

A tool for understanding how the current landscape will evolve.

**Horizon 1 (H1) — The present:** The current dominant paradigm. How does the world work today? What are the incumbents doing? What do customers expect?

**Horizon 2 (H2) — Emerging:** New approaches that are gaining traction but haven't replaced H1. Startups, new technologies, changing behaviors. This is where most venture investments live.

**Horizon 3 (H3) — Far future:** Radically different approaches that are barely visible today. Wild cards, science fiction becoming science, paradigm shifts. Pre-seed deep tech investments often bet on H3.

**Investment question:** Is this startup an H2 play (emerging, riding a clear transition) or an H3 play (far future, betting on a paradigm shift)? H2 is more predictable. H3 has higher upside but much higher risk.

## Weak Signal Detection

Weak signals are early indicators of large changes. They're easy to dismiss because they look marginal at the time.

### Where to Find Weak Signals

| Source | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| Academic papers | New research that could become commercial in 3-5 years |
| Patent filings | What are large companies patenting? What are they preparing for? |
| Regulation drafts | What are regulators starting to discuss? (Often signals what will be enforced in 2-3 years) |
| Fringe communities | What are early adopters and enthusiasts excited about? |
| Adjacent markets | What happened in similar sectors? (Fintech patterns often repeat in healthtech, etc.) |
| Geographic lead markets | What's happening in markets that often lead (US, China, Korea, Nordics)? |

### Signal Strength Assessment

| Strength | Description | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Noise** | Single anecdote, no pattern | Monitor casually |
| **Weak signal** | 2-3 independent data points suggesting a trend | Track actively |
| **Emerging trend** | Multiple signals from different sources converging | Factor into scenarios |
| **Established trend** | Widely recognized, data-supported | Factor into base case |
