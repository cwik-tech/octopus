# Shop Workflow & Milling

## Purpose

Sequence operations so stock remains stable, references stay reliable, and errors are discovered before expensive material is committed.

## Workflow

1. Review drawings and identify final dimensions, matched parts, grain requirements, and critical assemblies.
2. Acclimate stock as appropriate; inspect for twist, cup, bow, checks, knots, and grain issues.
3. Break down rough stock oversized and label parts before final milling.
4. Create one flat reference face, then one square reference edge; plane to thickness and rip or crosscut to final dimensions in a controlled order.
5. Batch like operations while retaining part identity and orientation marks.
6. Cut joinery after stock is stable and square; use test pieces first.
7. Dry fit assemblies before glue, confirming diagonals, reveals, hardware clearances, and clamp access.
8. Stage sanding and finishing so exposed faces remain protected and accessible.

## Accuracy Controls

- Reference every repeated cut from the same face or edge.
- Use stop blocks for repeated lengths; avoid measuring every duplicate part independently.
- Measure diagonals for squareness and check mating components together.
- Leave fitting allowance where a final hand-fit is preferable to a forced machine tolerance.

## Output

Provide an ordered operation list, material staging plan, reference-face convention, test-cut points, and quality gates.
