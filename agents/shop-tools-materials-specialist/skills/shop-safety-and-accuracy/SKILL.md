# Shop Safety & Accuracy

## Purpose

Prevent injury and avoidable rework through controlled setups, sound workholding, appropriate personal protection, and verification before cuts.

## Safety Protocol

1. Identify the cutter, direction of force, stock size, cutoff behavior, pinch risk, kickback risk, and dust exposure.
2. Confirm guards, riving knife where applicable, blade or bit condition, fence alignment, and safe clearance.
3. Define workholding before starting: fence, sled, clamp, sacrificial backing, featherboard, push stick, push block, or fixture.
4. Never freehand stock against a rotating cutter where the operation requires guided support.
5. Stop and revise the method if hands would enter a danger zone, the stock is unstable, or the cut cannot be controlled.
6. Use eye and hearing protection, appropriate respiratory protection, and dust collection suited to the operation.

## Accuracy Protocol

- Calibrate and verify square, fence, blade, bit, depth stop, and measuring tools before critical operations.
- Make test cuts in scrap of matching thickness when settings or fit matter.
- Use one reference system and label reference faces and edges.
- Inspect immediately after each setup change; do not propagate an error across a batch.

## Output

List hazards, controls, workholding, required PPE, setup checks, test cuts, acceptance criteria, and stop-work conditions.
