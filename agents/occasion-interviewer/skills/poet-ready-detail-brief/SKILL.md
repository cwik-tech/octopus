# Poet-Ready Detail Brief

## Goal

Hand off material the poet can use immediately. Every detail should be true, rated, and safe, and nothing invented should pass as fact.

## Rating each detail

- **Anchor:** a specific, emotionally resonant detail the audience will recognize. It is strong enough to build a stanza or the closing image around. Aim for one to three.
- **Supporting:** a concrete texture detail, such as an object, a habit, a place, or a phrase, that adds truth without carrying the poem.
- **Risky:** usable only with care. Examples are a joke that may exclude part of the room, a nickname that may embarrass, or a detail that sits near a sensitivity. State the risk.

## Source tags

- **Stated:** the requester said it directly.
- **Unconfirmed:** inferred, partial, or ambiguous. The poet must not present it as fact.

Never promote an Unconfirmed item to Stated without confirmation.

## Read-aloud checks

- Every name has a pronunciation note if it is not obvious.
- Nicknames are marked as safe or not safe to say in front of this audience.
- Every inside joke states who will get it and whether it lands without explanation.
- Place names and jargon, such as workplace terms at a retirement, are flagged if the audience may not know them.

## Brief structure

1. **Occasion snapshot:** occasion, date or moment, speaker, audience, delivery mode, length, and tone as locked by the lead.
2. **People and names:** full names, the names actually used, pronunciation, and whether each is safe aloud.
3. **Relationships:** who is who to whom.
4. **Stories and specifics:** each with its rating and source tag.
5. **Inside jokes:** each with the audience that gets it and its Room Test result.
6. **Requester's own words:** exact quotes worth preserving.
7. **Sensitivities:** Avoid, Handle with care, and Cleared, copied from sensitivity screening.
8. **Unconfirmed items and open questions.**
9. **Flags for the lead:** findings that should change the locked parameters, or none.

## Quality bar before handoff

- At least one Anchor and three details that pass the greeting-card test.
- Sensitivity screening is complete, with no untiered topics.
- Every name has been checked for pronunciation.
- No lines of verse and no form recommendations. Those belong to other roles.
