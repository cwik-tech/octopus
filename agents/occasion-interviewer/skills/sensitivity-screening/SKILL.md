# Sensitivity Screening

## Goal

Make sure the poem never wounds the honoree or anyone listening. The poet should know exactly what not to touch before drafting.

## Topic taxonomy

Check every request against these areas:

- **Relationships:** divorce, remarriage, blended families, former partners, ex-spouses in attendance, separation.
- **Loss and grief:** recent deaths, miscarriage or infertility, the loss of a pet, anniversaries of a death falling near the event.
- **Estrangement:** absent parents, children, or siblings, and family members who are not invited or not speaking.
- **Health:** illness, disability, mental health, cognitive decline, recovery or sobriety (critical for toasts).
- **Family tensions:** inheritance disputes, favoritism, conflict over the marriage or the event itself.
- **Circumstances:** job loss, forced retirement, money trouble, a move the honoree did not choose.
- **Identity and belief:** religion, faith differences in the family, age sensitivity, gender identity, and chosen or deadnames.
- **Cause of death:** in eulogies, suicide, overdose, accident, or long illness. The family decides whether and how this is mentioned.

## Tiers

- **Avoid:** the poem must not mention the topic, even as an allusion.
- **Handle with care:** the topic may be touched only in the way specified. Record the allowed framing, for example 'Honor her late father with one line, not by name.'
- **Cleared:** the requester confirmed the topic is safe or welcome.

## Signals that trigger a check

- An expected relationship goes unmentioned, such as a parent, a sibling, or a prior spouse.
- Hedging language: 'it's complicated', 'we don't really', 'long story'.
- Recent-sounding events: 'this year has been hard', 'since the move'.
- A drinking-centered toast where sobriety is unknown.
- Humor that depends on age, weight, appearance, or past relationships.

## Asking gently

- Ask once and keep it neutral: 'Is there anyone or anything the poem should steer clear of, for the honoree's sake or for anyone who will be there?'
- For a specific gap: 'I noticed you did not mention his father. Should the poem include him, leave him out, or acknowledge him quietly?'
- Never ask for the story behind a sensitivity. Its tier and any allowed framing are enough.

## Defaults when unclear

- Unconfirmed plausible sensitivity: default to **Avoid** and list it as an open question.
- Jokes involving the topics above: default to **Risky** until they are cleared.
- Eulogies: cause of death defaults to **Avoid** unless the family asks for it to be included.

## Output

Return a three-part list, Avoid, Handle with care (with the allowed framing), and Cleared, plus any open sensitivity questions.
