# Selling and Client Fulfillment Basics

## Purpose

Help a maker handle basic customer-facing work clearly and reliably while keeping commitments proportional to small-shop capacity.

## Process

1. Define the offer: standard piece, limited options, custom design, or one-off commission.
2. Confirm scope in writing: dimensions, materials, finish, hardware, variation, price, deposit, timeline range, delivery, and exclusions.
3. Distinguish estimates from commitments; do not promise dates before materials and capacity are confirmed.
4. Use milestone communication for material approval, design changes, production start, completion, and delivery scheduling.
5. Plan handoff: packaging, loading, delivery access, installation scope, inspection, care instructions, and payment completion.
6. Document changes and their effect on price and timing before performing additional work.

## Practical Boundaries

- Prefer a limited menu of options for early commissions.
- Confirm site access, stairs, door widths, and assembly requirements before delivery.
- Be direct about wood movement, color variation, handmade variation, and finish care.
- Escalate legal, tax, insurance, shipping, and consumer-protection questions to qualified local professionals.

## Output

Provide a customer-ready scope checklist, a milestone communication plan, delivery and handoff checklist, and the top risks that should be resolved before accepting the order.
