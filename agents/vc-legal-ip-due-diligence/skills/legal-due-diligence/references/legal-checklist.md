# Legal Due Diligence Checklist

## Purpose

Comprehensive checklist organized by category for legal due diligence on startup investments. Use selectively based on deal stage, sector, and identified risk areas.

---

## Corporate Formation & Governance

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation (or equivalent) — Delaware C-Corp is standard
- [ ] Bylaws — current, no unusual provisions
- [ ] Board resolutions — all major decisions properly authorized
- [ ] Board minutes — complete and up to date
- [ ] Good standing certificates — in state of incorporation and all operating states
- [ ] Organizational chart — legal entity structure (holding companies, subsidiaries)
- [ ] Qualified to do business in all required jurisdictions
- [ ] No pending or threatened dissolution or involuntary proceedings

---

## Cap Table & Equity

- [ ] Fully diluted cap table — reconciled and current
- [ ] Stock ledger — all share issuances documented
- [ ] Stock certificates or electronic records — complete
- [ ] Option grants — all properly authorized, with board approval
- [ ] 409A valuation — current (within 12 months), from reputable provider
- [ ] SAFE agreements — all executed, with clear conversion terms
- [ ] Convertible notes — all documented with maturity dates, caps, discounts
- [ ] Warrant agreements — all documented
- [ ] Founder stock purchase agreements — vesting schedules documented
- [ ] Stock restriction agreements — all founders and key employees
- [ ] Prior round documents — all previous investment agreements
- [ ] Rights and preferences — no unusual provisions from prior rounds
- [ ] Voting agreements — documented, no unusual provisions
- [ ] Right of first refusal / co-sale agreements — documented
- [ ] Investor rights agreements — documented
- [ ] Dead equity — quantified and plan to address if significant

---

## Intellectual Property

- [ ] IP assignment agreements — all founders, employees, and contractors
- [ ] Invention assignment clauses — in all employment/contractor agreements
- [ ] Patent applications — filed, pending, granted (by jurisdiction)
- [ ] Trademark registrations — filed, pending, granted
- [ ] Copyright registrations — if applicable
- [ ] Trade secret protection program — NDAs, access controls, documentation
- [ ] Open-source license compliance — bill of materials reviewed, no copyleft contamination
- [ ] Freedom to operate — FTO search conducted or opinion obtained
- [ ] Prior employer IP risk — founders' previous employment agreements reviewed
- [ ] University IP — license agreements if technology originated at a university
- [ ] Domain names — owned by the company (not individuals)
- [ ] Social media accounts — owned by the company

---

## Employment & Labor

- [ ] Employment agreements — all employees have signed agreements
- [ ] At-will employment — confirmed (or relevant jurisdiction terms)
- [ ] Non-compete agreements — any agreements from prior employers reviewed
- [ ] Non-solicitation agreements — in place for key employees
- [ ] Confidentiality agreements — all employees and contractors
- [ ] Contractor agreements — properly classified (employee vs. contractor)
- [ ] Worker classification compliance — no misclassification risk
- [ ] Benefits and compensation — market-rate, properly documented
- [ ] Equity compensation plan — properly adopted, with sufficient pool
- [ ] Key person dependencies — identified and risk assessed
- [ ] Pending employment disputes — lawsuits, claims, investigations
- [ ] Severance obligations — any existing commitments
- [ ] International employees — local labor law compliance verified

---

## Contracts & Agreements

- [ ] Customer contracts — standard terms, unusual provisions identified
- [ ] Revenue recognition compliance — contracts support claimed revenue
- [ ] Customer concentration — no single customer >25% of revenue
- [ ] Vendor/supplier agreements — key dependencies identified
- [ ] Partnership agreements — any strategic partnerships documented
- [ ] Exclusivity agreements — any exclusivity commitments
- [ ] Non-compete restrictions — any agreements restricting the business
- [ ] Lease agreements — office, equipment, data center
- [ ] Insurance policies — D&O (Directors and Officers), E&O (Errors and Omissions), general liability, cyber
- [ ] Government contracts — special compliance requirements if applicable

---

## Litigation & Disputes

- [ ] Active lawsuits — any pending litigation (plaintiff or defendant)
- [ ] Threatened claims — cease and desist letters, demand letters
- [ ] Regulatory investigations — any pending or past
- [ ] Patent infringement claims or risks
- [ ] Employment disputes — current or past
- [ ] Customer disputes — contract breaches, refund demands
- [ ] IP disputes — ownership, infringement, licensing
- [ ] Tax disputes — any pending with federal, state, or local authorities
- [ ] Litigation history — any resolved matters that reveal patterns

---

## Regulatory Compliance

### General
- [ ] Privacy policy — current, compliant with GDPR/CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) as applicable
- [ ] Terms of service — current, reviewed by counsel
- [ ] Data processing agreements — with all data processors
- [ ] Cookie consent — compliant with applicable law
- [ ] Accessibility compliance — ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) / WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) if applicable
- [ ] SOC 2 certification — completed or in progress
- [ ] Data breach notification procedures — documented

### Sector-Specific
- [ ] **Fintech:** Money transmitter licenses, AML/KYC (Anti-Money Laundering / Know Your Customer) program, SEC/FINRA (Financial Industry Regulatory Authority) compliance
- [ ] **Healthtech:** HIPAA compliance, BAAs with vendors, FDA pathway (if device/software)
- [ ] **Edtech:** FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) / COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) compliance, student data handling
- [ ] **AI/ML:** Bias testing, explainability documentation, emerging AI regulation compliance
- [ ] **International:** GDPR compliance, data localization requirements, cross-border data transfer mechanisms

---

## Tax

- [ ] Federal and state tax returns — filed and current
- [ ] Tax liens or disputes — none outstanding
- [ ] Sales tax obligations — registered and collecting where required
- [ ] International tax — transfer pricing, permanent establishment risks
- [ ] R&D (Research and Development) tax credits — properly documented
- [ ] 83(b) elections — filed by all founders and early employees who received restricted stock
