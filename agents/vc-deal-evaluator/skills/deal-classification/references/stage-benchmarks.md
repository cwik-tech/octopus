# Stage Benchmarks

Detailed metrics and expectations per funding stage. Use to calibrate whether a company's metrics match its stated stage and to identify companies that are over-classified (claiming later stage than metrics support) or under-classified (metrics ahead of stated stage).

Note: benchmarks shift with market cycles. These represent median ranges during normal capital market conditions. In frothy markets, medians inflate; in downturns, they compress.

---

## Pre-Seed

**Revenue:** $0. Possibly pre-product.

**Team:** 1-3 founders. No full-time employees beyond founders. May have advisors.

**Product:** Idea, prototype, or very early MVP. No production users.

**Traction signals:** Customer discovery interviews (20+ target customers talked to), waitlist signups, LOIs (Letters of Intent) from potential customers, design partners committed, hackathon/demo day feedback.

**Typical round:** $250K–$1.5M. SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) or convertible note. Valuation cap $3M–$10M.

**Dilution norm:** 10-15% on SAFE, uncapped or capped.

**Milestone to next stage:** Working MVP with first 5-20 users providing feedback. Evidence that the problem is real (not just that the solution is interesting).

**Evaluation emphasis:** Almost entirely team bet. Key question: "Do these founders have the unfair advantage (domain expertise, technical capability, network, obsession) to discover product-market fit in this space?"

## Seed

**Revenue:** $0–$500K ARR. Some seed companies are pre-revenue with strong usage signals.

**Team:** 3-10 people. Engineering-heavy. May have first non-founder hire.

**Product:** Working product with real users. Feature set may be narrow but core value proposition is testable.

**Traction signals:**
- Users: 100–5,000 active users (varies wildly by sector)
- Revenue: if SaaS, $5K–$50K MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue); if marketplace, GMV growing
- Engagement: DAU/MAU ratio, session frequency, feature adoption
- Growth: 10-20% month-over-month (good), 20%+ (exceptional)
- Retention: Month-1 retention >40% (consumer), >80% (B2B SaaS)

**Typical round:** $2M–$5M. Priced round or SAFE. Pre-money valuation $8M–$20M.

**Dilution norm:** 15-25%.

**Milestone to next stage:** Clear product-market fit signal — repeatable acquisition of customers who retain and pay. Series A readiness typically means $1M–$2M ARR with healthy unit economics.

**Evaluation emphasis:** Team (40%) + Product/Traction (30%) + Market (20%) + Terms (10%).

## Series A

**Revenue:** $1M–$5M ARR (SaaS). Marketplace: $5M–$25M GMV.

**Team:** 15-40 people. Functional leads in place (VP Eng, Head of Sales or Growth). Moving from generalists to specialists.

**Product:** Product-market fit demonstrated. Core product stable. Beginning to expand feature set or adjacent use cases.

**Traction benchmarks:**
- ARR: $1.5M–$4M (median), $5M+ (strong)
- Growth: 2-3x year-over-year (good), 3x+ (strong)
- NRR: 110%+ (good), 130%+ (exceptional)
- Gross margin: 60%+ (SaaS), 40%+ (marketplace)
- CAC payback: <18 months (good), <12 months (strong)
- Logo churn: <2% monthly (B2B), <5% monthly (SMB)
- LTV/CAC: 3x+ (good), 5x+ (strong)

**Typical round:** $8M–$25M. Pre-money valuation $25M–$80M.

**Dilution norm:** 15-25%.

**Milestone to next stage:** Proven unit economics, scalable go-to-market, organizational maturity for scale. Series B readiness typically means $8M–$15M ARR with clear path to $50M+.

**Evaluation emphasis:** Traction (35%) + Team (25%) + Market (20%) + Unit Economics (15%) + Terms (5%).

## Series B

**Revenue:** $8M–$30M ARR. Marketplace: $50M–$200M GMV.

**Team:** 50-150 people. Full executive team. Middle management layer emerging.

**Product:** Platform expanding. Multiple products or significant product breadth. Enterprise features (security, compliance, integrations).

**Traction benchmarks:**
- ARR: $10M–$25M (median), $30M+ (strong)
- Growth: 80-150% year-over-year (good), 150%+ (strong)
- NRR: 120%+ (good), 140%+ (exceptional)
- Gross margin: 65%+ (SaaS), 50%+ (marketplace)
- CAC payback: <15 months
- Burn multiple: <2x (efficient), <1.5x (capital-efficient)
- Rule of 40: Growth rate + profit margin > 40% (good), > 60% (strong)

**Typical round:** $20M–$60M. Pre-money valuation $80M–$300M.

**Dilution norm:** 10-20%.

**Milestone to next stage:** Path to profitability visible. Market leadership position or clear #2. Beginning to look like a company that could go public.

---

## Growth / Pre-IPO

**Revenue:** $50M+ ARR.

**Team:** 200+ people. Full C-suite. Board governance mature.

**Traction benchmarks:**
- ARR: $50M–$200M+
- Growth: 40-80% year-over-year
- Gross margin: 70%+ (SaaS)
- Operating margin: improving toward breakeven or positive
- Net dollar retention: 120%+
- Free cash flow: positive or clear line of sight

**Typical round:** $50M–$200M+. Pre-money valuation $500M–$5B+.

**Evaluation emphasis:** Financials (35%) + Market Position (25%) + Exit Path (20%) + Terms (15%) + Team (5%).

## Cross-Stage Red Flags

These signals suggest misclassification or trouble regardless of stage:

- **Metrics don't match fundraise:** Seed-stage metrics asking for Series A valuation
- **Growth without retention:** Revenue growing but cohort retention collapsing
- **Team/stage mismatch:** Solo founder at Series B, or 50-person team at Seed with no revenue
- **Capital inefficiency:** High burn with low growth (burn multiple >3x)
- **Fundraise timing desperation:** Running out of runway, forced to raise on unfavorable terms
