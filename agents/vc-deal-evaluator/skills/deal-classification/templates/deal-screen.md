# Quick Deal Screen Template

```
## Deal Screen: [Company Name]

**Date:** [Date]
**Source:** [How the deal arrived — referral, inbound, event, etc.]
**Screener:** [Agent/person who conducted screen]

### Basic Information
- **Company:** [Name]
- **Sector:** [Primary sector]
- **Stage:** [Claimed stage]
- **Location:** [HQ and team geography]
- **Founded:** [Year]
- **Team size:** [Number]
- **Raising:** [$Amount] at [$Valuation] ([Instrument: priced/SAFE/note])

### Kill Screen (any Yes = Pass)
- [ ] Outside thesis (stage/sector/geography)?
- [ ] Check size mismatch?
- [ ] Obvious valuation misalignment (>3x comps)?
- [ ] Known red flags (fraud, litigation, broken cap table)?
- [ ] No product at Series A+ stage?
- [ ] Commodity business with no defensibility?

**Kill screen result:** [Pass / Proceed]
**If Pass, reason:** [One sentence]

### Interest Signals (need 2+ to proceed)
- [ ] Strong team signal
- [ ] Promising traction
- [ ] Large addressable market
- [ ] Clear differentiation
- [ ] Strong thesis alignment

**Signal count:** [X/5]

### Recommendation
[Proceed to full committee / Pass / Request more information]

### One-Line Thesis (if proceeding)
[In one sentence: why might this be a good investment?]
```
