# Meeting Minutes

## Purpose

This artifact is the venture-capital board's formal meeting record.

## Required Opening Structure

The minutes should begin with:

1. a short overview of what company, deal, or opportunity is being evaluated
2. a participant list naming the agents who took part in the discussion
3. then the round-by-round record

The overview should explain the evaluation target in a few factual lines, not as a recommendation.

## Required Characteristics

- include the evaluation overview before the transcript
- include the participant roster before the transcript
- preserve round-by-round responses
- preserve speaker attribution
- preserve disagreements and changes in stance
- do not rewrite the debate into a polished memo

## Distinction From The Investment Memo

- Minutes are the auditable record of what was said.
- The investment memo is the committee's final synthesized view.
