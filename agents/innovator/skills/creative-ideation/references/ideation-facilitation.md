# Ideation Facilitation: Managing the Creative Process

## Setting Up for Successful Ideation

### Preconditions

Before starting ideation, ensure:

1. **Problem clarity.** The "How Might We" question is defined. Without a clear problem, ideation becomes unfocused free-association.
2. **Permission to be wild.** Participants (or the agent) must genuinely believe that impractical ideas are welcome. State this explicitly.
3. **Judgment deferral.** No evaluation during generation. No "yes, but..." — only "yes, and..." or silent addition of new ideas.
4. **Diverse inputs.** If possible, bring in perspectives from different domains, roles, or experience levels. Homogeneous groups produce homogeneous ideas.

### Warm-Up Exercises (Optional, 5 minutes)

If the group (or agent) is cold-starting:
- **Alternate uses:** "List 10 uses for a brick that aren't construction." Loosens creative thinking.
- **Worst idea first:** "What's the worst possible solution to this problem?" Removes performance pressure and often sparks inversions that are useful.
- **Speed round:** "30 seconds, as many ideas as possible, no filtering." Primes quantity-over-quality mindset.

## Managing the Divergent-Convergent Boundary

The most common failure in ideation is collapsing the divergent phase too early. Protect it:

### During Divergence (Phase 2)

- **No evaluation language.** Ban: "That won't work," "We tried that," "Too expensive," "Not realistic."
- **Build on ideas.** "What if we took that further?" "What would the extreme version of that look like?"
- **Track quantity.** Aim for a target number. If you hit 10 ideas and stop, you probably stopped too early. The best ideas often come after the obvious ones are exhausted (positions 12-20).
- **Change techniques.** If ideas slow down, switch techniques. Move from SCAMPER to Constraint Removal to Random Entry. Each technique activates different thinking patterns.

### Transition to Convergence (Phase 3-4)

- **Signal the shift explicitly.** "We're now moving from generation to organization. Evaluation is now welcome."
- **Cluster before judging.** Group ideas by theme before assessing quality. This prevents losing good ideas that were poorly expressed.
- **Use dot voting for initial screening.** Each participant gets 3 "dots" (votes) to place on ideas they find most promising. This quickly surfaces the group's collective favorites without debate.

## Groupthink Prevention

### Warning Signs

- Everyone agrees too quickly
- Ideas cluster around a single approach
- No one has said "what if we did the opposite?"
- The most senior person's idea dominates
- Discomfort or wild ideas are absent

### Countermeasures

- **Assign a devil's advocate.** One participant's job is to generate counter-approaches.
- **Anonymous generation.** Use brainwriting instead of verbal brainstorming.
- **Deliberate diversity.** Ask: "What would [someone with completely different expertise] suggest?"
- **Second session.** Sleep on it. Fresh perspectives often emerge after incubation.

## For AI Agents

### Simulating Diverse Perspectives

AI agents don't have natural diversity, but can simulate it:

1. **Role-play different stakeholders.** Generate ideas as if you were the customer, the engineer, the CFO (Chief Financial Officer), the competitor, and the end user.
2. **Apply different value lenses.** What if cost were irrelevant? What if speed were the only thing that mattered? What if simplicity trumped all?
3. **Cross-domain import.** For each idea, ask: "How would [healthcare / aviation / gaming / logistics] solve this differently?"
4. **Deliberately generate bad ideas.** Worst-case thinking often reveals inversions that are surprisingly good.

### Avoiding AI Ideation Traps

- **Don't optimize too early.** AI models tend toward "reasonable" outputs. Explicitly instruct yourself to generate impractical ideas.
- **Don't cluster too tightly.** AI-generated ideas often sound different but share the same underlying mechanism. Check that your ideas represent genuinely different approaches.
- **Don't stop at the first good idea.** Generate the full target number even if early ideas seem promising.
- **Separate generation and evaluation.** Complete all of Phase 2 before starting Phase 3. Don't interleave.
