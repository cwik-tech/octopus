### Session: 2026-04-01 — Deep research integration architecture for multi-agent pipeline template

- **What worked:** The HMW reframing at multiple levels of abstraction was highly effective. Moving from the narrow "how might we call a research API during execution" to the inverted "how might we make runtime research calls unnecessary" unlocked two genuinely distinct alternatives (pre-execution sprint, model-selection) that the rest of the board hadn't considered. The SCAMPER-style constraint removal ("what if the async problem doesn't exist?") also paid off — the Domain Researcher's empirical finding that named APIs are actually synchronous validated the constraint removal approach before anyone had to argue for it theoretically.
- **What didn't:** The diversity table (showing all three board positions share the same underlying mechanism) was the right move, but it landed after the Pragmatist and Contrarian had already anchored the framing. The mechanism analysis would have been more influential earlier in the session, before positions hardened. Also, Alternative 3 (accumulating knowledge base) was the weakest of the three — it answered the stated Question 3 but didn't escape the "research during execution" mechanism as cleanly as Alternatives 1 and 2.
- **Pattern noticed:** In technical architecture boards, the "false debate" pattern appears frequently: participants argue about implementation variants of the same approach (sync vs async wrapper, provider vs tool vs engine) while the genuinely different options go unexamined. The HMW reframing is the fastest tool for breaking this pattern — specifically the inverted HMW, which forces the question "what if the problem itself is optional?" The First Principles Fool and Pattern Matcher independently converged on the same structural insight (the board was solving a location problem, not a capability problem), which validates that the diversity check was correct.
- **For next time:** Lead with the mechanism diversity table before presenting alternatives. Naming that "all current proposals share mechanism X" is more persuasive than presenting novel alternatives cold — it creates the cognitive space for alternatives to land. Also: when the Domain Researcher provides empirical API data that invalidates a technical premise (async semantics don't exist for these APIs), pivot the entire alternative set immediately rather than presenting alternatives that still assume the invalidated premise. Alternative 1 did this well; Alternatives 2 and 3 could have been framed more sharply around the empirical finding.

### Session: 2026-04-04 — Michigan automotive market entry for Ubivis (foreign industrial tech vendor)

- **What worked:** The mechanism audit table was the right opening move and landed well — showing that every board recommendation (Denso call, VW referral, sales hire, website fix) shared the same underlying mechanism ("earn trust through relationships, then sell") created the cognitive space for genuinely different alternatives. The inverted HMW ("how might Ubivis make Michigan automotive buyers come to them?") was more generative than the narrow HMW and directly unlocked Alternative 3. The three developed alternatives (MMTC institutional legitimacy transfer, network-acquisition through talent hire, adjacent market beachhead) were mechanistically distinct — each bypassed a different failure point in the consensus strategy. The "adjacent market beachhead" option was particularly strong because it was immediately executable by the US team without HQ activation, which directly responded to the Client Lens's authority gap analysis. The closing question ("which of Ubivis's verticals is reachable without relationship capital?") reframed the target selection problem from brand alignment to reachability — a sharper axis.
- **What didn't:** The insurance carrier partnership (FM Global) idea was underdeveloped — flagged it for the Money-Maker rather than developing it fully. If that mechanism has genuine structural merit (carrier endorsement as trust transfer), it deserved the same four-part treatment as the other three alternatives. It appeared as an afterthought. Also, the MMTC alternative (Alternative 1) required the most domain-specific knowledge to evaluate — the Pragmatist and Domain Researcher would need to verify whether MMTC actually endorses commercial vendors or just facilitates connections. The plausibility claim needed more grounding.
- **Pattern noticed:** In market entry boards for foreign vendors entering relationship-gated ecosystems, the "relationship chain monoculture" pattern appears reliably: every agent converges on "activate the right relationship" as the mechanism, varying only in which relationship to activate. The diversity check is almost always productive in these sessions because the solution space almost always needs to expand beyond relationship transfer. The structural insight that consistently unlocks new options is the constraint removal question: "what if the key relationships never become available?" — this forces exploration of mechanisms that don't depend on any existing relationship capital. The Pattern Matcher's guild isomorphism analysis was highly complementary: their structural framing of "hedge craftsman route" and "letter of introduction route" as parallel tracks (not competing strategies) directly validated Alternative 3 (hedge craftsman = adjacent market beachhead) as the fallback track when HQ doesn't cooperate.
- **For next time:** When the Client Lens identifies a principal-agent authority gap early, design at least one alternative that is fully executable by the constrained party (US team without HQ activation) before developing alternatives that require the unconstrained party (Brazilian C-suite). In this session, the Adjacent Market Beachhead served that function, but it was Alternative 3 rather than Alternative 1 — it should have been first because it's the only option not blocked by the authority gap the Client Lens named. Sequence alternatives by executability given the actual authority constraints in the room, not by strategic elegance.
