## Backflow Triggers

Define in advance what findings would trigger iteration back to earlier agents or changes to the research agenda.

| Trigger | Finding | Action | Agent to Loop Back To |
|---------|---------|--------|----------------------|
| **Technical blocker discovered** | Gate 1 reveals architecture constraint we didn't anticipate | Halt market validation research; return to problem decomposition | Problem Decomposition |
| **Market assumption breaks** | Gate 2 survey shows pricing sensitivity much higher than assumed | Reprioritize positioning; iterate with Strategic Reframing before Gate 3 | Strategic Reframing |
| **Competitive threat emerges** | During Gate 2 research, discover competitor launched similar product | Trigger scenario planning for market consolidation; may require pivot | Scenario Planning |
| **Regulatory constraint identified** | Customer interviews reveal compliance requirement we missed | Escalate; may require iteration back to Domain Research or kill direction | Domain Research |

## Notes & Assumptions

- All timelines assume dedicated research capacity (1 FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) or equivalent).
- Budget estimates include external research (surveys, expert interviews) but not internal labor costs.
- If any gate produces a "Pivot" outcome, the next gate may be delayed or redesigned.
- Kill criteria are treated as binding — if triggered, we escalate the decision, not debate it.
