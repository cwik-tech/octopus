# Value-Add Assessment Skill

Capital is a commodity. What differentiates one investor from another is what they bring beyond money. This skill evaluates the fund's value-add for a specific deal.

## When to Use

On every deal where we compete for allocation. Especially important when multiple investors have term sheets and the founder is choosing.

---

## Value-Add Assessment Protocol

### Step 1 — Fund Capability Inventory

Map the fund's assets relevant to this specific company:

| Capability | Our Asset | Relevance to This Deal |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain expertise** | Partners/team with experience in this sector | [Specific value: can they advise on GTM, technology, regulation?] |
| **Portfolio synergies** | Existing portfolio companies that could be customers, partners, or knowledge sources | [Specific intros and partnerships available] |
| **Hiring network** | Access to executive talent, engineering talent, specialized roles | [Specific hires they'll need in 12 months that we can help with] |
| **Customer introductions** | Relationships with potential customers | [Specific enterprises or buyers we can introduce] |
| **Operational support** | In-house services (finance, legal, HR, marketing) | [What operational help does this company need?] |
| **Follow-on capital** | Ability to lead or participate in future rounds | [How much can we reserve for follow-on?] |
| **Brand/signal** | Our fund's reputation and signaling value | [Does our brand help this company recruit, sell, or fundraise?] |
| **Geographic reach** | Offices, networks, or expertise in relevant geographies | [Does the company plan to expand to markets where we have presence?] |

### Step 2 — Board Seat Value Framework

**The Board Seat Test:** If we take a board seat, what will we say in the first three meetings that the founders couldn't get from any other investor?

**High-value board contributions:**
- Specific operational advice based on experience scaling a similar company
- Introductions to 3-5 potential customers or partners within the first quarter
- Help recruiting a critical executive hire (VP Sales, VP Engineering, CFO)
- Guidance on navigating a regulatory landscape the fund has experience with
- Connecting to other portfolio founders who've faced the same challenges

**Low-value board contributions:**
- Generic strategic advice ("you should focus on growth")
- "Opening doors" without specific relationships
- Financial modeling and reporting (should be table stakes)
- Attending board meetings without contributing

### Step 3 — Competitive Investor Positioning

**Who else is competing for this deal, and what do they offer?**

| Competitor Fund | Their Edge | Our Edge | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Fund A] | [What they bring] | [What we bring] | [Who wins on value-add?] |
| [Fund B] | [What they bring] | [What we bring] | [Who wins on value-add?] |

**Our differentiation:** In what specific way is our value-add unique? If we can't articulate why the founder should choose us over another investor at the same terms, we don't have an edge.

### Step 4 — Founder Preference Research

**What do founders in this sector value from investors?**

This varies by stage and sector:
- **Early-stage founders:** Usually value advice, introductions, and operational support most
- **Growth-stage founders:** Usually value brand, follow-on capacity, and IPO experience most
- **Deep-tech founders:** Value patient capital, technical credibility, and regulatory expertise
- **Consumer founders:** Value brand knowledge, distribution partnerships, and marketing expertise

**Ask the founder:** "What are the top three things you're looking for in an investor beyond capital?" Then honestly assess whether we deliver on those three things.

### Step 5 — Post-Investment 100-Day Plan

Draft a concrete plan for the first 100 days after investment:

- **Day 1-30:** [Specific introductions, onboarding, first board meeting agenda]
- **Day 30-60:** [Key hire support, customer introductions, portfolio connections]
- **Day 60-100:** [Strategic planning support, operational improvements, milestone review]

This plan should be specific enough to share with the founder as part of the pitch for the deal.
