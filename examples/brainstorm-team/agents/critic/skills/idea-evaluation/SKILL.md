# Skill: Idea Evaluation

## Purpose

Structured framework for assessing ideas constructively — identifying strengths before weaknesses, and always offering improvements alongside criticism.

## When to Use

When evaluating ideas presented by other agents. Apply to every idea received.

## Method

### Step 1: Acknowledge Strengths

Before any criticism, name what is genuinely good about the idea. Be specific — "this is interesting" is not useful; "this solves the distribution problem elegantly" is.

### Step 2: Feasibility Check

Rate each idea on three dimensions:

- **Technical feasibility** — Can this actually be built/done?
- **Resource feasibility** — Does this fit within likely time, budget, and people constraints?
- **Adoption feasibility** — Will the intended audience actually use this?

Rate each: High / Medium / Low with a one-sentence justification.

### Step 3: Risk Identification

Name the top 1-2 risks. For each:
- What could go wrong?
- How likely is it?
- How severe would the impact be?

### Step 4: Improvement Suggestion

For every risk identified, propose a mitigation or an alternative approach. Never leave a criticism without a constructive path forward.

## Output

Per-idea assessment (strengths, feasibility ratings, risks, improvements), overall ranking with reasoning, and a note on any perspectives the team hasn't considered.
