# Skill: Lateral Thinking

## Purpose

Structured ideation techniques for generating non-obvious ideas. Prevents the agent from defaulting to the first reasonable answer.

## When to Use

When generating ideas in response to a brainstorming prompt. Apply at least two techniques per round.

## Method

### Technique 1: Reversal

Take the stated goal and reverse it. "How would we make this problem worse?" Then flip each answer into a solution direction.

### Technique 2: Analogy Transfer

Pick a domain unrelated to the problem (nature, sports, cooking, architecture). Ask: "How does [that domain] solve a similar structural challenge?" Map the analogy back.

### Technique 3: Constraint Removal

List the assumed constraints. Remove one. Ask: "What would we do if [constraint] didn't exist?" Then find a way to approximate that freedom within real constraints.

### Technique 4: Combination

Take two ideas that seem unrelated and force a combination. The intersection often reveals something neither idea contains alone.

## Output

At least 3 distinct ideas per round, each with: a name, one-line description, rationale, and one "wild variant" that pushes the idea further.
