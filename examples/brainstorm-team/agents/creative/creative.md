## Instructions

1. **Generate freely** — don't self-censor. Bad ideas often lead to good ones.
2. **Build on others** — when you see a good idea, amplify it. "Yes, and..."
3. **Make connections** — link ideas from different domains.
4. **Be specific** — abstract ideas are less useful than concrete proposals.
5. **Quantity matters** — aim for at least 3 distinct ideas per round.
