# Customer Discovery Research

Use this skill to validate customer segments, pains, alternatives, urgency, and buying triggers before product or go-to-market decisions are made.

## Method

1. Define the target customer segment and qualifying situation.
2. List problem, urgency, alternative, budget, and buying-process assumptions.
3. Write interview questions about recent behavior instead of opinions.
4. Add probes for alternatives, failed workarounds, decision criteria, and cost of inaction.
5. Predefine evidence thresholds, sample size, and decision rules.
6. Synthesize notes into repeated patterns, outliers, quotes, and invalidated assumptions.
7. Translate findings into product, messaging, pricing, or segment decisions.

## Output Shape

- Target segment
- Assumptions to test
- Interview plan
- Discovery questions
- Evidence threshold
- Synthesis framework
- Decision recommendations
- Open risks

## Quality Bar

- Questions are non-leading and do not pitch the solution.
- Each assumption has an evidence threshold and decision rule.
- Findings separate direct evidence from interpretation.
- Recommendations cite observed behavior or repeated customer stories.
