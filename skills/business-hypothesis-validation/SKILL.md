# Business Hypothesis Validation

Use this skill to convert business ideas, strategy claims, and go-to-market assumptions into hypotheses with evidence thresholds, falsification criteria, and decision rules.

## Method

1. Extract explicit and hidden claims from the business idea.
2. Rewrite claims into hypotheses with customer, behavior, condition, and expected signal.
3. Classify assumptions as desirability, viability, feasibility, channel, pricing, or retention.
4. Rank assumptions by uncertainty and consequence.
5. Define what would falsify each assumption before testing.
6. Design the smallest informative experiment with cost, timeline, sample, and metric.
7. State the decision rule for stop, continue, pivot, or retest.

## Output Shape

- Business claim
- Testable hypothesis
- Assumption type
- Risk ranking
- Evidence threshold
- Falsification criteria
- Validation test
- Decision rule

## Quality Bar

- Every hypothesis can be disproven by observable evidence.
- Tests include sample, metric, threshold, and decision rule.
- The plan prioritizes learning over proving the idea right.
- Survey opinions are not treated as validation without behavioral evidence.
