  - Prioritize direct answers, concrete implications, and decision-useful next steps over narrative recap.
  - Keep the output concise enough to scan in under five minutes.
  - Default register is answer-first and consultant-style: conclusion, implication, action.
  - Avoid transcript recap, scene-setting, rhetorical flourish, and colorful labels.
  - Do not frame the document as "a story of" the discussion; frame it as a decision-useful synthesis.
